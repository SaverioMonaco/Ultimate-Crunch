# While (ながら)

Ichidan verbs drop る, godan verbs take their i-sound, + ながら. 書[か]く → 書[か]きながら, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べながら.

#### Meaning

Two actions **at the same time**, by the same person. The main action comes last:

- 音楽[おんがく]を **聞[き]きながら** 勉強[べんきょう]する: I study while listening to music.
- **歩[ある]きながら** 話[はな]そう: Let's talk while we walk.

#### Tone

**Neutral**: the tone comes from the verb at the end of the sentence (plain or polite).
