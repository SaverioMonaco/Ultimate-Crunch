# Polite past negative (ませんでした)

The polite past negative: ません + でした. 食[た]べません → 食[た]べませんでした.

#### Meaning

A **past** action that did **not** happen:

- 昨日[きのう]は 雨[あめ]が **降[ふ]りませんでした**: It didn't rain yesterday.
- 週末[しゅうまつ]は どこにも **行[い]きませんでした**: I didn't go anywhere at the weekend.

#### Tone

**Polite**: used with strangers, colleagues, shop staff and people older than you. The safe choice when in doubt.
