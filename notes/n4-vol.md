# Volitional (意向形[いこうけい])

Godan verbs take their o-sound + う, ichidan verbs drop る + よう. 書[か]く → 書[か]こう, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べよう.

#### Meaning

**Let's** (casual), or with と 思[おも]う, **I think I'll**:

- 一緒[いっしょ]に **行[い]こう**: Let's go together. (invitation)
- 明日[あした]から 運動[うんどう]を **しよう**と 思[おも]う: I think I'll start exercising tomorrow. (intention)

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you. The polite version is ましょう.
