# Negative (ない形[けい])

The plain negative: the verb's a-sound + ない. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べない, 書[か]く → 書[か]かない.

#### Meaning

A **present** or **future** action that does **not** happen:

- 肉[にく]を **食[た]べない**: I don't eat meat. (habit)
- 明日[あした]は 学校[がっこう]に **行[い]かない**: I won't go to school tomorrow. (future)

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
