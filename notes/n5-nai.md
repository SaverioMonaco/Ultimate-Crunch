# Negative (ない形[けい])

The plain negative: ichidan verbs drop る, godan verbs take their a-sound, + ない. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べない, 書[か]く → 書[か]かない.

#### Meaning

A **present** or **future** action that does **not** happen:

- 果物[くだもの]は**売[う]らない**: They don't sell fruits. (habit) <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/14081634">Tatoeba #14081634</a>
- **教[おし]えない**よ: I won't tell you. (future) <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/8823050">Tatoeba #8823050</a>

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
