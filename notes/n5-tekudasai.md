# Please do (てください)

The polite request: the て-form + ください. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べてください, 書[か]く → 書[か]いてください.

#### Meaning

Asking someone **to do** something:

- ここに 名前[なまえ]を **書[か]いてください**: Please write your name here. (instruction)
- ちょっと **待[ま]ってください**: Please wait a moment. (request)

#### Tone

**Polite**: used with strangers, colleagues, shop staff and people older than you. Among friends, drop the ください: 待[ま]って.
