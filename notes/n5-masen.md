# Polite negative (ません)

The polite negative: ichidan verbs drop る, godan verbs take their i-sound, + ません. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べません, 書[か]く → 書[か]きません.

#### Meaning

A **present** or **future** action that does **not** happen:

- タバコは **吸[す]いません**: I don't smoke. (habit)
- 今日[きょう]は 会社[かいしゃ]に **行[い]きません**: I won't go to the office today. (future)

#### Tone

**Polite**: used with strangers, colleagues, shop staff and people older than you. The safe choice when in doubt.
