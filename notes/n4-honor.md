# Honorific (尊敬語[そんけいご])

お + the ます stem + になる, or a special verb. 書[か]く → お 書[か]きになる, 食[た]べる → 召[め]し 上[あ]がる, 行[い]く → いらっしゃる.

#### Meaning

Raises **the person who does the action**: a teacher, a customer, your boss. Never used about yourself:

- 先生[せんせい]が 本[ほん]を **お 書[か]きになる**: The teacher writes a book.
- 社長[しゃちょう]は 何[なに]を **召[め]し 上[あ]がります**か: What will the president have?

#### Tone

**Very polite** (keigo): for customers, bosses, teachers and other people you must show respect to. Usually followed by ます.
