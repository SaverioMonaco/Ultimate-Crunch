# Polite past (ました)

The polite past: ます → ました. 食[た]べます → 食[た]べました.

#### Meaning

A **past** action, already done:

- 昨日[きのう] 映画[えいが]を **見[み]ました**: I watched a movie yesterday.
- 朝[あさ]ご 飯[はん]を **食[た]べました**: I had breakfast.

#### Tone

**Polite**: used with strangers, colleagues, shop staff and people older than you. The safe choice when in doubt.
