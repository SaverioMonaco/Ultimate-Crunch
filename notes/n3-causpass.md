# Causative-passive (使役受身形[しえきうけみけい])

Godan verbs take their a-sound + される (す verbs: させられる), ichidan verbs drop る + させられる. 書[か]く → 書[か]かされる, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べさせられる.

#### Meaning

**Be made to** do something, usually against your will. With feelings, **be made to feel**:

- あの 子[こ]には 興奮[こうふん]**させられる**ぜ: That girl just drives me wild. <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/230979">Tatoeba #230979</a>

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
