# Please don't (ないでください)

The negative request: the ない form + でください. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べないでください, 書[か]く → 書[か]かないでください.

#### Meaning

Asking someone **not** to do something:

- ここで 写真[しゃしん]を **撮[と]らないでください**: Please don't take pictures here. (rule)
- 心配[しんぱい] **しないでください**: Please don't worry. (reassurance)

#### Tone

**Polite**: used with strangers, colleagues, shop staff and people older than you. Among friends, drop the ください: 心配[しんぱい]しないで.
