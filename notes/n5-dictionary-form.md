# Dictionary form (辞書形[じしょけい])

The form a verb has in the dictionary and in the vocabulary lists: 食[た]べる, 書[か]く, する.

#### Meaning

A **present** or **future** action, not yet done:

- 毎日[まいにち] 本[ほん]を **読[よ]む**: I read books every day. (habit)
- 明日[あした] 東京[とうきょう]に **行[い]く**: I'll go to Tokyo tomorrow. (future)

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
