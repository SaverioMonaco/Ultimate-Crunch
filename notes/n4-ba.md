# ば-conditional (ば形[けい])

Godan verbs take their e-sound + ば, ichidan verbs drop る + れば. 書[か]く → 書[か]けば, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べれば.

#### Meaning

**If**: a condition and what follows from it, often something always true:

- **急[いそ]げば** 間[ま]に 合[あ]う: If you hurry, you'll make it.
- 春[はる]に **なれば** 桜[さくら]が 咲[さ]く: When spring comes, the cherry trees bloom. (always true)

#### Tone

**Neutral**: the tone comes from the verb at the end of the sentence (plain or polite).
