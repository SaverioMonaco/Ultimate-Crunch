# Past negative (なかった)

The plain past negative: ない → なかった, like an い-adjective. 食[た]べない → 食[た]べなかった.

#### Meaning

A **past** action that did **not** happen:

- 昨日[きのう]は 何[なに]も **食[た]べなかった**: I didn't eat anything yesterday.
- 宿題[しゅくだい]を **しなかった**: I didn't do my homework.

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
