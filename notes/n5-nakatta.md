# Past negative (なかった)

The plain past negative: ない → なかった, like an い-adjective. 食[た]べない → 食[た]べなかった.

#### Meaning

A **past** action that did **not** happen:

- 誰[だれ]も**来[こ]なかった**: Nobody came. <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/5093">Tatoeba #5093</a>
- 何[なに]も**書[か]かなかった**: I didn't write anything. <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/2101577">Tatoeba #2101577</a>

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
