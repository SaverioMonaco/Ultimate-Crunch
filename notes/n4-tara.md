# たら-conditional (たら形[けい])

The た form + ら. 書[か]く → 書[か]いたら, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べたら.

#### Meaning

**If**, or **when / after**: the first action happens, then the second. On its own, 〜たら？ is a casual **why don't you**:

- **見[み]たら**教[おし]えて: If you see, let me know. (if) <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/10657902">Tatoeba #10657902</a>
- **着[つ]いたら**電話[でんわ]しろよ: Call me when you get there. (when) <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/11095678">Tatoeba #11095678</a>
- ちょっと**休[やす]んだら**？: Why don't you rest a bit? (suggestion) <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/4260188">Tatoeba #4260188</a>

#### Tone

**Neutral**: the tone comes from the verb at the end of the sentence (plain or polite). The most common conditional in speech.
