# Past (た形[けい])

The plain past: built like the て-form, with た / だ in place of て / で. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べた, 書[か]く → 書[か]いた, 読[よ]む → 読[よ]んだ, 行[い]く → 行[い]った.

#### Meaning

A **past** action, already done:

- 昨日[きのう] 映画[えいが]を **見[み]た**: I watched a movie yesterday.
- もう 宿題[しゅくだい]を **した**: I already did my homework.

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
