# Polite (ます形[けい])

The polite form: the verb's i-sound + ます. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べます, 書[か]く → 書[か]きます.

#### Meaning

A **present** or **future** action, not yet done:

- 毎朝[まいあさ] コーヒーを **飲[の]みます**: I drink coffee every morning. (habit)
- 来週[らいしゅう] 京都[きょうと]に **行[い]きます**: I'll go to Kyoto next week. (future)

#### Tone

**Polite**: used with strangers, colleagues, shop staff and people older than you. The safe choice when in doubt.
