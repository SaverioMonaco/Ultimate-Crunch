# Prohibition (禁止形[きんしけい])

The dictionary form + な. 書[か]く → 書[か]くな, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べるな.

#### Meaning

A blunt **don't!**:

- **触[さわ]るな**!: Don't touch!
- **心配[しんぱい]するな**: Don't worry.

#### Tone

**Rough**: heard from men among close friends, in sports and on signs. Rude to say to anyone else: use ないでください.
