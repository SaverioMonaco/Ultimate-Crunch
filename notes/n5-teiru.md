# Ongoing (ている)

The て-form + いる. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べている, 書[か]く → 書[か]いている.

#### Meaning

An action **in progress**, or a **state** that lasts after a change:

- 今[いま] 本[ほん]を **読[よ]んでいる**: I'm reading a book now. (in progress)
- 兄[あに]は 結婚[けっこん] **している**: My older brother is married. (state)

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you. In speech the い often drops: 読[よ]んでる.
