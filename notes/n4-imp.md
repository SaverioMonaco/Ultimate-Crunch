# Command (命令形[めいれいけい])

Godan verbs take their e-sound, ichidan verbs drop る + ろ. 書[か]く → 書[か]け, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べろ.

#### Meaning

A blunt **order**:

- 警察[けいさつ]を**呼[よ]べ**！: Call the police! <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/10574448">Tatoeba #10574448</a>
- おい、**待[ま]て**よ！: Hey, wait up! <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/1321505">Tatoeba #1321505</a>

#### Tone

**Rough**: heard from men among close friends, in sports, on signs and in emergencies. Rude to say to anyone else: use てください.
