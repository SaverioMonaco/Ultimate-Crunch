# Command (命令形[めいれいけい])

Godan verbs take their e-sound, ichidan verbs drop る + ろ. 書[か]く → 書[か]け, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べろ.

#### Meaning

A blunt **order**:

- 早[はや]く **起[お]きろ**!: Get up already!
- **止[と]まれ**: Stop. (road sign)

#### Tone

**Rough**: heard from men among close friends, in sports, on signs and in emergencies. Rude to say to anyone else: use てください.
