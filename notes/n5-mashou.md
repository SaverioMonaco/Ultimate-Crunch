# Polite, let's (ましょう)

The polite volitional: ichidan verbs drop る, godan verbs take their i-sound, + ましょう. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べましょう, 書[か]く → 書[か]きましょう.

#### Meaning

**Let's** do something together, or (with か) **shall I / shall we**:

- 一緒[いっしょ]に 昼[ひる]ご 飯[はん]を **食[た]べましょう**: Let's have lunch together. (invitation)
- 窓[まど]を **開[あ]けましょう**か: Shall I open the window? (offer)

#### Tone

**Polite**: used with strangers, colleagues, shop staff and people older than you. The safe choice when in doubt.
