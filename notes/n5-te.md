# て-form (て形[けい])

The connecting form: ichidan verbs drop る + て, godan verbs change their last sound + て / で. 食[た]べる → 食[た]べて, 書[か]く → 書[か]いて.

#### Meaning

Joins actions in order: **and (then)**. On its own, a casual **please**:

- 朝[あさ]ご 飯[はん]を **食[た]べて**、学校[がっこう]に 行[い]く: I eat breakfast and go to school.
- ちょっと **待[ま]って**: Wait a moment. (request)

It is also the base of many other forms: ている, てください, てもいい, ...

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual) when used alone as a request: with close friends and family, or with people younger than you. Joining actions, it takes the tone of the verb at the end of the sentence.
