# Passive (受身形[うけみけい])

Godan verbs take their a-sound + れる, ichidan verbs drop る + られる. 書[か]く → 書[か]かれる, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べられる.

#### Meaning

Something **is done** to the subject. Often the subject suffers it (the "suffering passive"):

- この 本[ほん]は 多[おお]くの 人[ひと]に **読[よ]まれる**: This book is read by many people.
- 電車[でんしゃ]で 足[あし]を **踏[ふ]まれた**: Someone stepped on my foot on the train. (suffering)

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you. For ichidan verbs it looks the same as the potential: 食[た]べられる is "is eaten" or "can eat".
