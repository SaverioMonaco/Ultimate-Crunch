# Potential (可能形[かのうけい])

Godan verbs take their e-sound + る, ichidan verbs drop る + られる. 書[か]く → 書[か]ける, 食[た]べる → 食[た]べられる.

#### Meaning

**Can** do something. The object usually takes が instead of を:

- 漢字[かんじ]が **書[か]ける**: I can write kanji.
- 刺身[さしみ]が **食[た]べられる**: I can eat sashimi.

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you. In speech, ichidan verbs often drop the ら: 食[た]べれる.
