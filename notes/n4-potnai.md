# Potential, negative (可能形[かのうけい]の 否定[ひてい])

The potential with る → ない. 書[か]ける → 書[か]けない, 食[た]べられる → 食[た]べられない.

#### Meaning

**Cannot** do something:

- トムは**泳[およ]げない**: Tom can't swim. <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/4591663">Tatoeba #4591663</a>
- 君[きみ]を**忘[わす]れられない**: I can't forget about you. <a class="src" href="https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/5975119">Tatoeba #5975119</a>

#### Tone

**Plain** (casual): used with close friends and family, or with people younger than you.
